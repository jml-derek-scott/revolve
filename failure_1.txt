Stage A did not fail. The four Sim2 images are a good empty-dress product shot. Stage B failed on shape: Hunyuan3D-2mv was given a hollow, layered, sheer garment on pure white, and the mesh it returned is not that dress. Fit and bake then did their jobs on a bad shell, which is why the clearance numbers look fine and the model still looks wrong.

What actually broke
The dress is three layers plus two 1-pixel straps: opaque bandeau, sheer midriff, short opaque slip, sheer midi overlay with a high slit. Hunyuan was asked for one watertight 80k shell (remove_background: true) from the Sim2 front and back. That shell is closed (0 boundary edges) but topologically a sponge (Euler −26, lots of tunnels). After fit it sits at midi height (y 0.28–1.46 m) with SDF energy 6.8e-11. So placement and collision passed. The garment is still not the product.

What is worth researching
Sheer-on-white vs Hunyuan. Grey underlay, no remove_background, or a filled “ghost body” that is not a person. Anything that stops white fabric reading as empty. Until that is solved, this dress will keep coming back with chest holes.

Whether Hunyuan is the wrong shape tool here. It wants a single opaque object. This product is layered cloth + hairline straps + a slit. A garment reconstructor, a pattern/drape path, or separate shells (bandeau, slip, overlay) are the live alternatives. One 80k watertight mesh is probably a dead end for this SKU.

Straps as geometry vs straps as a later strip. Do not expect Hunyuan to emit 2 mm tubes. If straps matter, they are a post step (curves, two quads, or a dedicated mask).

Do not spend the next cycle on SDF, TELEA, or ControlNet flanks. Collision already cleared. Bake already painted almost everything. The mesh is the problem; better side inpaint will not grow straps or close a midriff tunnel.

Decision you still owe the pipeline: for a midi with its own slit, is the garment allowed to be “mannequin + projected photos” (T1 fitted-shell, no real overlay), or must overlay/slit be real geometry? Right now we have no third option, and both existing ones fail this dress in different ways.
